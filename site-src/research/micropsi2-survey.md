## Abstract

This survey examines the theoretical foundations and software architecture of MicroPsi2, Joscha Bach's implementation of Dietrich Dorner's Psi theory of motivated cognition. We situate MicroPsi2 within the broader landscape of machine consciousness research, surveying Global Workspace Theory, Integrated Information Theory, Attention Schema Theory, predictive processing, and higher-order theories. We then assess the alignment between Socio-Psi — a Jungian archetypal cognitive architecture for situated AI — and the Psi theoretic tradition, identifying both convergences and divergences that inform the project's future direction.

## Key Highlights

!!! info "Psi Theory's Modulator Layer"
    The most distinctive feature of Psi theory: modulators (arousal, resolution, selection threshold, securing rate, goal valuation) shape *how* the agent thinks, not just *what* it wants. Emotions emerge from modulator configurations — anger is high arousal + low resolution + competence frustration.

!!! info "Six Consciousness Theories Surveyed"
    Global Workspace Theory, Integrated Information Theory, Higher-Order Theories, Attention Schema Theory, Predictive Processing / Active Inference, and Embodied / Enactive approaches. Each is assessed for computational implications and relevance to Psi theory.

!!! info "COGITATE Results (2025)"
    The first large-scale adversarial test of GNW vs. IIT (published in *Nature*) challenged key predictions of *both* theories, pushing the field toward theory revision rather than winner-take-all competition.

!!! info "Jungian Computation: A Missing Thread"
    Despite natural mappings from Jungian concepts to computational structures (Persona → social policy, Shadow → suppressed tendencies, Self → global integration objective), Socio-Psi appears to be the first system to implement archetypal dialogue as a functional cognitive subsystem in a situated agent.
