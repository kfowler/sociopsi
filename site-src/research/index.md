## Theoretical Foundations

The project draws on two traditions:

1. **Psi theory** (Dorner, 2003; Bach, 2009, 2012) — an agent architecture where behavior emerges from homeostatic drives, modulators, and spreading activation.
2. **Jungian analytical psychology** (Jung, 1968; Major, 2025) — a model of the psyche as dialogue between archetypes (Shadow, Anima, Persona, Self) mediated by a conscious Ego.

The [system architecture paper](sociopsi-architecture.md) describes how these traditions are welded onto a concrete platform. The [competitive analysis](competitive-analysis.md) evaluates alignment with MicroPsi2. The [survey](micropsi2-survey.md) situates Socio-Psi within machine consciousness research.
